# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free open-source community for everyone regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

- Be constructive, factual, and kind in code reviews, issues, and discussions.
- Respect maintainers' legal boundaries: never share or request copyrighted ROM files or proprietary dumps.
- Focus criticism on technical evidence and reproducible test cases, never on people.

Project maintainers may moderate or remove comments and contributions that violate these standards.
